FAQ

Common questions.

Everything you need to know about SafeTag 24x7.

Q What is SafeTag 24x7?

SafeTag is a QR-based emergency identity tag. When scanned, it immediately shows your emergency contacts, blood group, medical conditions, and other critical information to first responders or bystanders.

Q Does the scanner need an app?

No. Any smartphone camera — iPhone or Android — can scan the QR code directly. No app download, no login. Your information appears instantly in any browser.

Q Who can use SafeTag?

Anyone! Riders, students, working professionals, senior citizens, children — SafeTag is designed for every Indian. If you or someone you love travels, SafeTag is for you.

Q Is my data private and secure?

Yes. Only the information you choose to share is visible when scanned. Your profile is encrypted and you control exactly what is displayed in an emergency.

Q Can I update my emergency profile?

Absolutely. Log in to your SafeTag account from any device to update your medical information, contacts, or any other details at any time. Changes take effect immediately.

Q How durable are the tags?

All SafeTag products are waterproof and built for outdoor use. The helmet tag has a strong adhesive, the keychain is made of durable metal, and all QR codes are UV and scratch-resistant.
